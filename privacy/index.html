Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Deskup is a desktop application for Windows. This policy explains what data Deskup handles and where it goes. In short: Deskup has no servers. We never receive, store or see your data.

Data Deskup stores

Deskup stores what you create in the app, such as workspaces, tasks, habits, focus sessions, notes, boards, whiteboards, clipboard history and preferences, in a file on your own computer.

If you use Deskup without signing in

All data stays on your computer. Deskup does not send it anywhere.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. When you sign in, Deskup asks Google for:

  • Your name, email address and profile picture, to show which account is signed in and to identify you to your teammates.
  • Access to Google Drive, used only to: create a Deskup folder in your Drive and save your Deskup data there, so it syncs between the computers where you sign in; create team folders when you create a team, share them with the people you invite, and list who has access; and read and write Deskup files inside team folders other people shared with you.

Deskup does not read, change or delete any other files in your Google Drive. Your sign-in tokens are kept on your computer, encrypted with your operating system's secure storage. Data travels directly between your computer and Google; it never passes through servers run by Deskup.

You can sign out in Deskup at any time, and revoke Deskup's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the Deskup folder in your Drive deletes your synced data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Deskup's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Deskup does not sell this data, does not use it for advertising, does not transfer it to anyone except to provide the features described above, and does not use it to train AI or machine learning models.

Teams

When you put items in a team, they are saved in that team's Google Drive folder, and everyone in the team can see them, together with your name, email address and the name of the computer you synced from.

Updates

Deskup checks GitHub for new versions. These requests contain no personal data beyond what any web request includes, such as your IP address, which GitHub handles under its own privacy policy.

Children

Deskup is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on the Deskup releases page.

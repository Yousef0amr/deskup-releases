Privacy by architecture

Deskup never hosts your data.

Start locally on day one. Sign in with Google only when you want personal multi-PC sync or shared Team folders inside your own Google Drive.

Team SpacesGoogle Drive → Deskup/Teams/<team-folder>/

Create a team, invite members by email or deskup-team:// code, and switch spaces in one click. Only items assigned to that team ever sync to teammates.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does Deskup store my notes, tasks, or boards on your servers?

No. Deskup has no backend database. Everything is stored locally on your PC, and when you enable sync, Deskup reads and writes only inside your own Google Drive.

How does Personal vs. Team privacy work?

Workspaces, boards, whiteboards, and notes each belong either to Personal or to a specific Team. Switch spaces globally from the sidebar, and Personal items—plus tasks, habits, focus history, and clipboard—never upload to any team.

Why does Windows SmartScreen warn when I first run the installer?

Deskup’s installer is open-distributed directly from GitHub Releases and does not yet have a paid EV code-signing certificate. Click “More info → Run anyway” once after downloading.

How do updates work after I install Deskup?

Deskup checks the latest release feed automatically in the background and downloads new installers seamlessly, applying updates cleanly on restart.
